# Surface brief — hub (index.html)

## Scope & mode
The corpus hub, single surface. Mode: Persuade (visitor finds their app and clicks through; secondary: credibility).

## Audience, job, action
Mac users hunting a useful native utility; peers/recruiters reading craft. Action: open a project landing page. Proof: 25 real shipped projects, signed & notarized, 0 telemetry, MIT.

## Chosen direction (user-locked, 2026-08-22, seed 39f91a6c, challenger won)
**Layered sticker accretion / flight case** (vernacular-ephemera-layered-sticker-accretion). The hub is a road case owned by one builder: 25 projects as die-cut vinyl stickers accumulated over years, depth = chronology (newest on top, oldest bleached), controls are stickers (active one lifts a corner), stenciled case markings carry the thesis, a taped packing manifest carries findability. Clarity wins conflicts: sticker names stay legible; the manifest is the catalog backbone.

## Memorable moment
Hovering a sticker peels its corner; the language switch is three small stickers on the case.

## Constraints
- Trilingual EN/FR/ZH-Hant via body-class mechanism (binding).
- All claims factual (21/14/07/01/04 counts, no telemetry, MIT, EN/FR app localization).
- WCAG AA floor may not regress. Zero external images; all texture authored in CSS/SVG.
- base.css stays the shared socle.
