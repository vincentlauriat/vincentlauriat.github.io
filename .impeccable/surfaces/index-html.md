# Surface brief — hub (index.html)

## Scope & mode
The corpus hub, single surface. Mode: Persuade — visitor finds their app and clicks through; peers judging craft weigh equally.

## Audience, job, action
Mac users hunting a useful native utility; peers/recruiters reading craft (equal weight, user 2026-10-12). Action: open a project page. Proof: 36 shipped works, 22 native Swift instruments, signed & notarized, 0 telemetry, MIT.

## Chosen direction (user-pinned, 2026-10-12)
**Architect's monograph.** The hub is one author's book of works: title page, contents, index of works (No. 01–36), four chapters opened by full-bleed cobalt pages with a roman numeral, each work a numbered plate (its own pictogram redrawn on a construction grid) with a caption. Light grey paper, ink, one cobalt; Schibsted Grotesk only; 12-column grid. Build path: code-led. Light chosen by the builder (user delegated): the monograph's natural register, daylight desk reading.

## History
Flight case (2026-08-22) replaced as too loaded/costume and slow to search; a teletext round (2026-10-12) was built and rejected by the user as not convincing ("plus élégant, architecte").

## Memorable moment
Each plate is plotted once, a left-to-right pen pass, as it enters the screen.

## Constraints
- Trilingual EN/FR/ZH-Hant via body-class mechanism (binding).
- Not AI-looking, not a dev portfolio, no speed sacrifice (one 47 KB font, no images on the page).
- WCAG AA floor may not regress.

## Unresolved
- Whether project landing pages adopt the monograph (out of scope).
